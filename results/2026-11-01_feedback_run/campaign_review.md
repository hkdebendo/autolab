## Campaign review

**All three computational finalists are selenium-substituted sulfides that tied their parent in lithium diffusion and passed lithium-metal passivation, led by Li6P2SeS7 with energy above the hull of -0.007 eV/atom.**

### What the campaign taught

- Round 1 combined chlorine, oxygen, and sodium ideas, but Li5P2S7Cl showed chlorine diffusing at 17.7 % of lithium against a 5 % limit, Li14P6S21O showed oxygen at 5.3 % of lithium, and NaLi13(P3S11)2 was non-passivating against lithium metal with a reaction of 2.440 eV/atom.
- After those failures round 2 shifted to isovalent selenium and arsenic plus magnesium vacancies: Li14P6SeS21 tied its parent in diffusion against the parent at 1.34 ± 0.34 with an intact framework, while Li4Mg(PS4)2 was non-passivating with a reaction of 2.271 eV/atom.
- Round 3 corrected the halogen dose toward a single larger bromine and concentrated isovalent selenium and arsenic on Li3PS4, giving Li13P6S21Br tied in diffusion against the parent at 1.01 ± 0.20, Li3PSeS3 at energy above the hull of -0.013 eV/atom, and Li12As3PS16 at -0.014 eV/atom.
- The chemical idea that held was isovalent selenium for sulfur, which reached the final in both parents, whereas sodium and magnesium cation mixing repeatedly failed lithium-metal contact and chlorine or oxygen anion substitution repeatedly risked framework motion.

### What worked

- Li6P2SeS7 passed as passivating, tied its parent in diffusion against the parent at 0.66 ± 0.21, and had energy above the hull of -0.007 eV/atom, band gap recorded predictions of 2.49 eV and 3.10 eV, and a stable voltage window from 1.8 V to 2.24 V.
- Li12P4SeS15 also tied its parent in diffusion against the parent at 0.82 ± 0.25, had energy above the hull of -0.004 eV/atom, and had conductivity of 5.66 mS/cm at 300 K compared with 1.22 mS/cm for its parent under the same protocol.
- Li7P3SeS10 tied its parent in diffusion against the parent at 1.07 ± 0.23, was passivating, and had conductivity of 133.68 mS/cm at 300 K compared with 111.39 mS/cm for its parent under the same protocol.

### What failed

- Li5P2S7Cl failed lithium diffusion testing because the framework moved, with chlorine diffusing at 17.7 % of lithium against the 5 % limit; a hypothesis is that double sulfur-to-chlorine substitution with two lithium vacancies left chlorine weakly bound and mobile with lithium.
- Li14P6S21O failed lithium diffusion testing because the framework moved, with oxygen diffusing at 5.3 % of lithium against the 5 % limit; a hypothesis is that isolated dilute oxygen on a sulfide site without full phosphate-like coordination was under-bound and followed lithium motion.
- NaLi13(P3S11)2 and Li4Mg(PS4)2 failed contact with lithium metal as non-passivating, with reactions of 2.440 eV/atom and 2.271 eV/atom; a hypothesis is that sodium and magnesium were reduced by lithium metal to metallic sodium or lithium-magnesium products rather than a passivating interphase.

### Next campaign

> Find selenium- or arsenic-substituted sulfide solid electrolytes derived from Li3PS4 or Li7P3S11 that tie or exceed their parent in lithium diffusion and remain passivating against lithium metal.

Starting from: Li3PS4, Li7P3S11.

- Test lithium diffusion at 600 K and lithium-metal contact for the untested round-3 candidates Li3PSeS3 and Li12As3PS16 from Li3PS4.
- Probe dilute mixed isovalent substitutions on Li3PS4 combining selenium for sulfur with arsenic for phosphorus at one to two substituted sites per cell, avoiding sodium, magnesium, chlorine, and isolated oxygen.
- Retest a single bromine-for-sulfur substitution with one lithium vacancy on Li3PS4, following Li11P4S15Br tied diffusion against the parent at 1.15 ± 0.28 and Li13P6S21Br at 1.01 ± 0.20.

Selenium substitution produced all three finalists and tied parent diffusion across both parents, while arsenic on Li3PS4 reached energy above the hull of -0.014 eV/atom without slow-test confirmation. Sodium, magnesium, concentrated chlorine, and isolated oxygen instead failed by non-passivating lithium-metal reactions of 2.112 eV/atom to 2.507 eV/atom or by framework motion above the 5 % limit.
